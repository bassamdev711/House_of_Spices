# House of Spices Marketing Research

## Store URL

https://house-of-spices-linl.vercel.app/

## Observed store identity and messaging

The live homepage is titled: "بيت البهارات - حيث تتحول النكهة إلى مذاق". The visible navigation includes الرئيسية، المجموعة، من نحن، تجربة بيت البهارات، تواصل معنا، تتبع الطلب، المفضلة، البحث، وسلة المشتريات. The current palette is deep olive green with warm light accents and Arabic RTL typography. The storefront presents categories including توابل عالمية، بهارات القهوة والشاي، بدائل صحية، وزعفران فاخر. The core promise is purity, authenticity, premium sourcing, and a sensory experience. Product cards visibly show prices in Yemeni rials and product discovery CTAs.

The homepage includes a newsletter signup, contact form, social links, order tracking, and a footer. This supports a sales narrative around a store owner getting a ready-made branded storefront, direct customer acquisition, digital ordering, measurable product discovery, and owned customer touchpoints.

## Conversion narrative

The deck will sell the platform as a growth system for spice retailers: premium brand presentation, searchable product catalog, QR-to-store packaging, direct ordering, discounts and campaigns, inventory-aware checkout, order tracking, and a mobile-first customer experience. The central CTA will be: "حوّل كل عبوة إلى نقطة بيع".
